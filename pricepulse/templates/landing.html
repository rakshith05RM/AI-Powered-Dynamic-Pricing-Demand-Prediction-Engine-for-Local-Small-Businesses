Common questions

What kind of businesses is this for?

Grocery stores, bakeries, restaurants, cafes, clothing and electronics shops, pharmacies, salons and other local retail or service businesses.

How much sales history do I need?

At least 30 sales records to train an initial model — the more history you have, the more accurate the forecasts become.

Can I control how much prices can change?

Yes. You set minimum and maximum prices per product, and the platform enforces configurable maximum increase/decrease limits.

Is my business data shared with other businesses?

No. Each business owner only ever sees their own products, sales, forecasts and recommendations.
